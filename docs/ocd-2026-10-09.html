甲基基因組Wide關聯研究揭示強迫症的表遺傳學特徵與性別差異

Biological psychiatry · Methylome-Wide Association Study of Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder.

全樣本最大規模之OCD甲基組研究發現35個DMP與17個DMR，涉及神經傳導與發育相關基因，並顯著受遺傳變異調節，提示精準醫學之可能性。

Biological psychiatry · Compulsive Coping Behavior, Developed Predominantly by Sign-Trackers, Is Exacerbated by Chronic Atomoxetine.
